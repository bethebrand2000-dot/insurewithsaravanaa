Health insurance claim support

On the hardest day, one phone call to someone who knows what to do.

Before the hospital

Save your insurer's helpline, your policy number and the list of network hospitals near you. I can help you set this up in a few minutes.

During the hospital stay

I help you inform the insurer, request cashless treatment where it applies, and understand what is and is not payable, so you can focus on your family.

After discharge

I help you collect and submit documents for reimbursement and follow up with the insurer. If a claim is reduced or disputed, I explain your options, including the insurer's grievance process and IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal.

What I check with you

  • Policy number and helpline saved
  • Network hospitals near you
  • All bills, reports and discharge summary
  • Written reason for any rejection
  • Grievance steps if needed

Common questions

Do you help with claims on policies bought elsewhere?

My claim support is for policies I service. For others I am happy to look at your documents and point you to the right process.

What if my claim is rejected?

Ask for the reason in writing. We can then approach the insurer's grievance cell and, if needed, IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal or the Insurance Ombudsman.
