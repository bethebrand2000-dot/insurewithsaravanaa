Questions

Things people ask me first

How much will health insurance cost me?

It depends on your age, the sum insured, the plan and your health history. On our first call I can explain the options and what drives the price, so you can decide what fits your family and budget.

Do I pay you anything for your advice?

No. You pay only the insurer's premium. My remuneration comes from the insurer under IRDAI rules, and I am happy to explain it if you ask.

I have diabetes or blood pressure. Can I still get cover?

Often yes, though there may be a waiting period or a higher premium. What matters most is telling the insurer honestly at the start, so your claim is protected later.

Is there an age limit for buying health insurance?

IRDAI's 2024 directions say insurers cannot refuse a health policy purely because of age, where earlier many stopped at 65. Premiums and waiting periods still apply, so starting early still helps.

What happens on the day of a claim?

Call me. I help you inform the insurer, request cashless treatment where it applies, and collect the right documents, so you can focus on the person in the hospital bed.

Do you only help people in Villupuram?

No. I am based in Villupuram, but I advise families across India by phone, WhatsApp and video call, and most paperwork can be completed digitally. Distance is not a barrier.
