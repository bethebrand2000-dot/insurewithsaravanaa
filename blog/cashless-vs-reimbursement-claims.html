Cashless or reimbursement: which claim route should you use?

By Rtn. S. Saravanakumar · 9 November 2026 · 4 min read

When a family member is admitted to hospital, one of the first questions is whether to use cashless treatment or pay and claim later.

Cashless

At a network hospital, the insurer settles the eligible bill directly with the hospital. You pay only the non-covered amount. For a planned admission, request approval in advance. For emergencies, inform the insurer as soon as possible.

IRDAI's 2024 master circular directs insurers to decide on a cashless request within one hour, and to give the final authorisation at discharge within three hours.

Reimbursement

You pay the hospital, then submit the bills and documents to the insurer. This is the route if you are treated at a non-network hospital. Keep every original bill, report and receipt.

Which is better?

  • Choose cashless when the hospital is in the network. It protects your savings at a stressful time.
  • Use reimbursement when the right hospital is not in the network. Treatment quality comes first.
Before you need it: check which hospitals near you are in your plan's network, and save the insurer's helpline number in your phone.

Some items, such as certain consumables, may not be payable under either route. Your policy document lists them.
