Why health insurance claims get rejected, and how to avoid it

By Rtn. S. Saravanakumar · 9 November 2026 · 5 min read

Most claim problems are not surprises. They follow patterns that you can see in advance.

1. A condition was not disclosed

Leaving out a health condition, even a mild one, is the most serious cause of disputes. Declare everything when you apply.

2. The treatment fell inside a waiting period

If the illness is covered only after a waiting period, a claim before that date can be refused. Know your dates.

3. The treatment is excluded

Every policy has exclusions. Read them before you buy, not after the hospital visit.

4. Late information or missing documents

Inform the insurer as soon as you can, and keep every bill, report and discharge summary. Delays in paperwork cause many of the avoidable problems.

5. The claim is reduced, not refused

Room-rent limits, sub-limits and co-payments can cut the payout without rejecting it. These are the most common surprises.

If a claim is rejected, ask for the reason in writing and approach the insurer's grievance cell. If unresolved, you can use IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal or the Insurance Ombudsman.
